## chatbot-maatje

Asset: `assets/chatbot-landing/images/chatbot-maatje.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent hero cutout for a Dutch Softora chatbot landing page. Primary request: A delightful little round burgundy fuzzy creature with two expressive ivory oval eyes, tiny dark pupils, tiny rounded hands and large ivory sneakers. It wears an oversized matte peach headset with a little microphone, waves one hand hello, and holds a sculptural ivory speech bubble with three burgundy raised dots in the other hand. Beside the creature float two small rounded chat bubbles in pale peach and sage, one with a burgundy heart symbol, one with three ivory dots. Character stands on a pale peach rounded oval plinth. Premium tactile 3D editorial art direction: fine burgundy fur, soft cream clay, matte peach ceramic, beautiful ambient shadows and realistic warm studio light. Curious, capable, cheerful personality, sophisticated grown-up playful aesthetic, not a robot. Palette deep wine #85234f, coral #ee8669, peach #f2c6ac, ivory #f8f5ef, sage #bccab5. Square composition, entire subject and floating bubbles fully visible, generous transparent breathing room, no crop. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND with alpha, only a subtle soft shadow below the plinth. No words, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks.
